# LANGPIB-WCH: hand-off

**State (2026-11-04):** routed, 4 layers, DRC clean, 0 unconnected, ERC 0; production files, cost and enclosure done;
final pre-order review: no blockers. Ordered 5 at JLC 2026-11-04 as is (D1 at CPL rotation, Y1 flagged "difficult", pending review).
Plan and decisions: [plan.md](plan.md). Everything is generated by kicad-tools `projects/langpib-wch/` (README there has the
rebuild order); `Hardware/style_lint.ignore` lists the reasoned lint leftovers.

**References:** J1 USB-C, J2 HR911105A MagJack (JLC-fitted THT), J3 GPIB plug (DNP, hand-soldered), J4 1x7 SDI + UART
header (DNP), U1 CH32V407RET6, U2 SRV05-4, U3 AMS1117-3.3, R1 / R2 CC, R3 status LED, R4 / R5 jack LEDs, R6 ATN
pull-down, C19 / C20 MDI centre taps, D1 status LED, SW1 DFU.

**Pin map:** DIO1-8 = PB13 / PB15 / PB10 / PB9 / PB12 / PB14 / PB11 / PB8 (one GPIOB read + 256-entry table); REN PA15,
EOI PC10, DAV PC11, NRFD PC12, NDAC PD2, IFC PB3, SRQ PB4, ATN PB5; DFU PD8, status LED PA8; PHY LEDs PD14 (green, J2.9)
/ PD15 (yellow, J2.12) with `ETHPHY_LED_RM`; USB PA11 / PA12; SDI PA13 / PA14; USART1 PA9 / PA10. Spare: PA1, PA2, PC13-15.
MDI pairs and polarity are swapped at the jack (Auto-MDIX + polarity detection): firmware must leave Auto-MDIX on.

**Placement (2026-11-04, user):** board 57.15 x 46.2. J2 (139.9, 114.5, 90) and J1 (152.2, 125.2, 90)
on the right edge. J3 (116.7, 133.3). U1 (114, 118.5, 180), 2 mm north of
the first routing. U3 (133, 124.1, 90): tab north, pins south, +5V caps C3 / C2 east on the trunk, +3V3 caps C4 / C6 / C5
west on one F.Cu +3V3 area. SW1 (106, 101.9) and D1 (102.2, 107.6) top left.

**Routing (by hand, `route_pre.py`):** In1 GND, In2 +3V3 (0.12 clearance so J3's pin rows keep plane necks), GND pours on
F/B, per-pad GND vias, 3 mm stitching grid, teardrops.
- MDI on F.Cu from pins 28-31 north through the decap channel, east to J2 (no vias); C19 / C20 in the pocket between the
  TX legs. PHY LEDs up U1's west side, east below SW1 through R4 / R5, into the jack between its pin column and shield pin.
- GPIB controls F.Cu fan to J3's top row; DIO via staircase under U1, B.Cu fan; USB F.Cu round J3's west and south side.
- DFU: short B.Cu hop under the PHY LED pair to SW1; LED_N F.Cu to D1; SWD / NRST on B.Cu, UART on F.Cu to J4.
- +3V3: U3 area -> trunk west at y 125.6 -> C13 / pin 64 and the pin 1 feed; other supply pins via their cap to In2.
- Reviews: round 3 (Ethernet, MCU + GPIB zones) and round 4 (whole board, after the U1 / U3 / corner rework) fixes are
  in: C13's own GND via, plane necks at J3, one GND via for C4 / C5 / C6 (none in C5's pad gap), U3 pin 2 inside the +3V3
  area. User: MDI pairs 0.6 apart (gap 3x width), F.Cu GND fill round and under U1 (no pin-ring keepout on the LQFP).
  Left as is: 2 x 10u ceramic out caps (design block), SW1 / D1 near the edge.

**Firmware (2026-11-04):** phase 0 written and building, DFU bootloader included and embedded in the app
([firmware.md](firmware.md)); nothing has run on a CH32V407 yet. Toolchain: xPack riscv-none-elf-gcc in `~/opt`.

**Bring-up (2026-11-21), board 1:** J4 and J3 fitted. Supplies ok (VDDK 1.2 V, VDD/VDDA 3.3 V). SDI works once
the probe switches the chip from its power-up 1-wire mode to 2-wire (Reference-WCH programmer). D1 lights (the +x
placement rule holds); `FEATURE_SIGN` bit 0 = 1, both jack LEDs work; the internal D+ pull-up works (USB enumerates);
Auto-MDIX and polarity correction on (`MDI, polarity reversed`, 100M). With J3: USB still enumerates (the pair under
J3 is fine) and the GPIB pin self-test passes; an HP 3456A and an HP 66332A pass every transport. Details and the
remaining checks: [firmware.md](firmware.md) "Still to verify".

**Hardware review (2026-11-21, read-only), no blocking fault.** Respin: route USB D+/D- off the J3 seating area (F.Cu,
x 107.6-108.8 under the west block; the plug body is plastic, so it is safe on this board); MDI pairs are polarity-
inverted as well as swapped (10BASE-T TX relies on the partner's polarity correction); AMS1117 margin on a weak 5 V
(needs > ~4.4 V in); a DNP SWIO pull-up footprint. Bench: J1 D+/D- to GND after fitting J3; 5 V through 3k into a
J3 pin with the board unpowered (FT pins at VDD = 0 unspecified); both USB-C orientations.

**Production (2026-11-21):** boards 2-5 flashed with `make production`, all pass (log in `production-log.csv`):
langpib-3b03, -718b, -0c68, -c981. All five run v0.2.0 (2026-11-21): board 1
over the network, boards 2-5 over USB DFU with SW1 (the app starts USB only once an instrument is found). Board 1 is
langpib-92ce; network updates are open (no password set).
